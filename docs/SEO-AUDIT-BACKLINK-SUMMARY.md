# Backlink quality audit — sanitized summary (TASK 75, 2026-10-25)

Public summary; raw GSC/Ahrefs exports are private (see R-035 in [SEO-ROADMAP.md](SEO-ROADMAP.md)).

**Method:** GSC link exports verified; linking pages fetched and checked live (status, rel attribute, context); GSC manual-actions and security reports; Google's official link-spam and disavow guidance; free Ahrefs Backlink/Traffic Checker for Space Glass and sclass.od.ua. The full Ahrefs tool was unavailable (monthly limit until 2026-11-03), so some metrics are UNKNOWN.

**Findings**
- GSC shows 9 linking domains. About 96% of the linked pages come from two sources: an automatic website-review tool (many language pages) and the owner's own personal site. Independent links are few (directories, one industry library, a ranking page, an old agency client list).
- Manual actions: none. Security issues: none.
- Classification: KEEP — own personal site, company directory profile, industry-library article (nofollow), social profile; MONITOR — auto tool pages, ranking listing, old agency client list, one dead domain; INVESTIGATE — one dofollow paid-placement directory article (payment status unknown, owner question R-047); removal candidates 0; disavow candidates 0.
- SEO-spam pages (`.shop/.store/.xyz`) visible in Ahrefs appear for the competitor too, are not in GSC's link report, and do not justify a disavow under Google's guidance.
- Ahrefs free values on 2026-10-25 (different tool variant than the baseline; low–medium confidence): Space Glass DR 23 (jump from ~5 unverified), 554 referring domains, ~66 dofollow, organic traffic estimate 37; SCLASS DR 10, 677 referring domains, ~61 dofollow, traffic estimate 191. Link parity is close; the organic-traffic gap (~5×) is not explained by backlinks alone.
