## Required structure for every commercial page
- Unique H1, title and meta description.
- Commercial intro focused on the search intent.
- Product/solution types.
- Materials, glass thicknesses, fittings and colour options.
- Installation and delivery geography.
- Pricing factors without invented fixed promises.
- Process from request to installation.
- FAQ block and FAQPage schema.
- BreadcrumbList schema.
- Internal links to category, city, project and knowledge pages.
- CTA to calculator/request form.

## SEO rules
- No doorway-style duplicate text between cities.
- City pages are only for services with real local installation intent.
- Products deliverable nationwide stay on nationwide pages.
- Ukrainian copy is the canonical primary source.
- RU/EN/DE must be proper translations, not mixed-language placeholders.
