# Space Glass — approved site architecture

## Core page types
- Homepage
- About
- Services hub
- Service category
- Catalog
- Product category
- Product page
- Projects hub
- Project detail
- City landing pages: Kyiv, Lviv, Odesa
- Knowledge Hub
- Article / FAQ detail
- Contacts

## SEO linking model
Product → category → project → city → article → FAQ → related product.

## Rules
- FAQ on every commercial page.
- Ukrainian is primary.
- Russian is secondary.
- Local services: Kyiv, Lviv, Odesa.
- Shippable products: all Ukraine.
- White background dominates.
- Header and footer are black.
- Brand mint is used for CTA, numbers, accents and line icons.
- No duplicate city pages for products delivered nationwide.
